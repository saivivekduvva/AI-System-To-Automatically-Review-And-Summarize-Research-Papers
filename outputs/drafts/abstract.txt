## Academic Abstract

This study investigates university students' perceptions regarding the integration of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) technologies, such as ChatGPT, within higher education settings. A quantitative survey was administered to 399 undergraduate and postgraduate students across various disciplines in Hong Kong to gauge their familiarity, willingness to engage, perceived benefits, and associated challenges. The findings reveal a generally positive attitude toward adopting GenAI for teaching and learning. Students highly value GenAI's potential for personalized learning support, assistance with writing and brainstorming, and enhanced research capabilities. Correlation analysis demonstrated that perceived willingness to use GenAI is positively linked to both knowledge of the technology ($r = 0.189; p < 0.001$) and frequency of use ($r = 0.326; p < 0.001$). Conversely, concerns regarding factual inaccuracy were significantly higher among infrequent users. These insights underscore the necessity for educators and policymakers to develop informed guidelines that address student concerns while strategically leveraging GenAI's capacity to enhance efficiency and learning outcomes.
