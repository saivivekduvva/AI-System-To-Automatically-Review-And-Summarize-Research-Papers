## Academic Abstract

This study investigates university students' perceptions regarding the integration of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) technologies, specifically tools like ChatGPT, within higher education settings. A quantitative survey administered to 399 undergraduate and postgraduate students across various disciplines in Hong Kong assessed familiarity, willingness to engage, perceived benefits, and associated challenges. The findings indicate a generally positive disposition towards utilizing GenAI in teaching and learning. Students identified significant potential in areas such as personalized learning support, assistance with writing and brainstorming, and enhanced research and analysis capabilities. Furthermore, correlation analysis revealed that perceived willingness to use GenAI is positively linked to both knowledge of the technology ($r = 0.189; p < 0.001$) and frequency of use ($r = 0.326; p < 0.001$). Conversely, concerns persist regarding factual inaccuracy and the potential undermining of educational value. These insights are crucial for educators and policymakers aiming to develop informed guidelines for the responsible and effective implementation of GenAI tools to enhance future learning experiences.
